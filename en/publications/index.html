    Apparatus and method for controlling take-off and landing pad structure using hexapod mechanism

    헥사포드 매커니즘을 이용한 이착륙 패드 구조물의 제어 장치 및 방법

    Dong-Wook Lee, Sung-Min Kim, Dong-Kyu Lee · Korea National University of Transportation Industry-Academic Cooperation Foundation

    Application
    10-2023-0182029 (2023.12.14)
    Publication
    10-2025-0091757 (2025.06.23)
    International
    PCT international application (WO2025127829 A1)

    When an eVTOL aircraft prepares to land, the system predicts the touchdown impact load from aircraft information and adjusts the spring stiffness and damping of six suspensions arranged as a hexapod to reduce shock and vibration on the vertiport take-off and landing pad.
