About the lab

Our lab combines analysis and experiments on unmanned aerial vehicles and UAM systems. We build multibody dynamics and flight dynamics models and validate the results with test rigs and measurement equipment that we build ourselves.

We are also developing systems that use stereo cameras, UWB, LiDAR and other sensors to measure and monitor the position of UAM aircraft taking off from and landing at vertiports, and we analyze how vertiport structural vibration caused by rotor downwash affects the measurement accuracy of these systems.

Research

We work in three areas: flapping-wing aerial vehicles, vision-based UAM surveillance systems, and UAM electric propulsion.
